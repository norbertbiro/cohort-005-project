# Team Purchases are refunded per Seat, not as a whole

A Team Purchase is one payment for several Seats, but each Seat is used by a different Student. So we judge and refund each Seat on its own. A Seat that passes the refund checklist (14 days from the Purchase, under 2 hours watched and under 50% of lessons completed by the Student who claimed it, no open Refund Request) can be refunded for its equal share of the amount paid. A Seat that fails stays with the team. The Team Admin asks for one or more Seats in one Refund Request, and an Admin approves or declines that request as a whole.

## Considered Options

- **Whole Purchase only, blocked once any Seat is claimed** (the code before this decision). Rejected: one busy Student blocks the refund for every unused Seat.
- **Whole Purchase only, never per Seat**. Rejected for the same reason.

## Consequences

- A refunded Seat can never be claimed again. If it was claimed, that Student's Enrollment ends.
- The watch and lesson checks are measured on the Student who claimed the Seat, never on the Team Admin who paid.
